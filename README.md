I operate at the intersection of **embedded intelligence, autonomous systems and applied artificial intelligence**. From crafting low-level firmware for microcontrollers to designing multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures, I focus on transforming abstract ideas into robust, deployable solutions.

> **💡 Core Philosophy:** I believe in building systems that solve real-world problems. Whether it's an autonomous disaster-response rover or an accessible web platform for dyslexic readers, technology should be engineered with precision and deployed with purpose.
