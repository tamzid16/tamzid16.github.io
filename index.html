About Me - BEHIND THE SYSTEMS!

MD TAMZID HOSSEN OMI● LIVE

A B.Sc. graduate in IoT and Robotics Engineering from University of Frontier Technology, Bangladesh, operating at the intersection of embedded intelligence, autonomous systems and applied artificial intelligence. As a co-author of an IEEE research paper on an autonomous disaster-response rover and a researcher contributing to JuteRAG, I explore how intelligent technologies can address complex and real-world challenges.

My engineering practice spans publicly released repositories, encompassing retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) platforms, web and mobile applications, FastAPI services and embedded systems. From low-level firmware to intelligent software architectures, I focus on transforming abstract ideas into robust, integrated and deployable solutions.
